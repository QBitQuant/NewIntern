Cheshire, Massachusetts

A Berkshire County dairy town — est. 1793 · pop. 3,258 (2020)

Founding

Incorporated in 1793. Settled in the late 1760s by Baptists from Rhode Island who named it for its English namesake, Cheshire.

The Dairy Country

Successful dairy farming took early hold — the rolling pastures you see below were put to work feeding the herd.

Rail Trail

The former Boston & Albany branch line now runs as part of the Ashuwillticook Rail Trail, threading right through town.

Pittsfield MSA

Part of the Pittsfield, MA Metropolitan Statistical Area in the northernmost corner of Berkshire County.
